How to use these floors

  • Breville runs the same $500-class sales three or four times a year. November 2026 may not beat them — the win is catching the return to the floor if the price lifts first.
  • Ninja and Philips lows land at the sale events themselves, not between them.
  • The Philips 3200's floor kept dropping after Thanksgiving 2025 ($450, then $398 in mid-December) — a Black Friday price is not always the floor, and our "lowest tracked" labels never freeze at the event price.
  • We never tag a deal "lowest" at full list price. A sale price above last year's floor is usually worth waiting on.
