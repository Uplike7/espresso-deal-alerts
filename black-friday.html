Is it better to buy espresso gear on Black Friday or Cyber Monday?

Buy machines on Black Friday week; save Cyber Monday for grinders, accessories, and beans. Retailers front-load their deepest machine discounts on November 27, then rotate smaller-ticket categories through Monday, November 30.

Are Black Friday espresso prices actually the lowest of the year?

Usually not. The strongest machine discounts launch on Black Friday itself, while Cyber Monday leans toward accessories, grinders, and beans. If a tracked machine hits its 30-day low during Black Friday week, that is typically the moment to buy.
