Buyer’s FAQ

How much should I spend on my first espresso machine?

Under $500 gets you real espresso; the $500–$1,000 tier is the enthusiast sweet spot with PID temperature stability and commercial 58mm parts. Spend the rest of your budget on the grinder.

Is the grinder really more important than the machine?

Yes — consistently. Grind size uniformity controls extraction quality more than any machine feature. A $300 grinder with a $500 machine outperforms a $300 grinder-free setup at double the price.

When is the best time to buy espresso gear?

Black Friday and Q4 clearance are historically the deepest discounts in this category. We check prices every hour, 24/7, and alert our Telegram channel the moment a tracked product drops.

Should Canadian buyers buy from US retailers?

Watch currency conversion, duty, and shipping — a US “deal” can cost more than CAD retail. Our site labels every price with its currency (CAD/USD) so you can compare honestly.
