About Espresso Deal Alerts

What this is: Espresso Deal Alerts is a small, independent price-tracking project for home espresso gear. It is not a store and sells nothing itself — it watches retailer prices so buyers don't have to. Contact: support@espressodealalerts.com.

What we do

Espresso Deal Alerts is an independent price-tracking service for home espresso enthusiasts. We monitor the public product catalogs of five specialty retailers — Whole Latte Love, Seattle Coffee Gear, Clive Coffee, Chris' Coffee and iDrinkCoffee — and check every price several times a day. Every price on the site is pulled directly from the retailers' own product feeds; drop alerts are verified against our own price history.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links: if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Retailers never pay us to change a price or a ranking — prices come from their live feeds and are displayed as-is. Prices shown in CAD are marked CAD (iDrinkCoffee lists in Canadian dollars).

Editorial independence

We do not accept payment for placement, we do not run sponsored posts, and we do not fabricate urgency. If a "sale" price is higher than a previous tracked price, we say so.
