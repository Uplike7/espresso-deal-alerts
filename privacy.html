Privacy Policy

Who runs this site: Espresso Deal Alerts is operated by One Smart Umbrella, an independent Ontario studio. Questions about this policy or your information: support@espressodealalerts.com.

What we collect

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It records page views, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at a page. It does not give us your name or your exact address.

Email alerts. If you subscribe to price-drop alerts, we collect the email address you give us — and nothing else. Subscribing is always optional.

What we never collect. Espresso Deal Alerts has no accounts, sells nothing itself, and never handles payment information. We do not ask for or store payment card details, passwords, or government identification.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies so it can tell a returning visit from a first visit and measure the site's traffic in aggregate. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the tracker works the same either way.

Some links on this site are affiliate links. When you click one and land on a retailer or an affiliate network's site, that retailer or network may set its own cookies to attribute a purchase — those cookies are set and governed by their sites and their privacy policies, not by us. We explain how affiliate links work on our About page.

How we use information

Analytics data is used only to operate and improve the tracker — which pages and products people actually use, and whether the site is working. Email addresses are used only to send the alerts you asked for. We do not use your information for advertising profiles, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

Sharing

We share information only in these limited ways: with Google, as our analytics provider, in the aggregate form described above; and with affiliate networks and retailers only in the sense that clicking an affiliate link takes you to their sites, where their own policies apply. We never sell your email address or analytics data, and we would only disclose information if required by law.

Retention and your choices

You can unsubscribe from email alerts at any time using the unsubscribe link in any alert email, and your address is then removed from the alert list. You can control or clear cookies through your browser. To ask what information we hold about you, or to request its deletion, email support@espressodealalerts.com and we will take care of it. Analytics data is kept only in the aggregated reporting Google Analytics provides.

Children

This site is a shopping tool for adults buying coffee equipment. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date below. The version published here is always the current one.
